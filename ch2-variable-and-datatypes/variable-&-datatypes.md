## Variable 

Variable is the container in which we can store values.

There are 3 ways of declaring variable:
1. `var`
2. `let` 
3. `const`

| `var` | `let` | `const`|
|-----|-------|------------|
|Can be declared without assignment. | Can be declared without assignment. | Cannot be declared without assignment. |
|Re-declaration and re-assignment is possible. | Re-declaration is not possible but re-assignment is possible. | Neither re-claration nor re-assignment is possible. |
|Can be accessed before declaration. | Cannot be accessed before declaration. | Cannot beaccess before declaration|
|It has global scope. | It has block scope. | It has block scope. |
